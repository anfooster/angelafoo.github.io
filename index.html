03 / ABOUT ME

Engineering with
hands-on intent.

Throughout my engineering experiences, I've developed a strong appreciation for the connection between design and manufacturing. From designing and machining tooling to working on production processes and building complex assemblies, I've learned how valuable it is to understand not just how something should function, but how it can be manufactured efficiently and reliably.

My experiences at EBR Systems, BMW Manufacturing, and Cal Poly Racing have reinforced the importance of working closely with different teams and learning from the people directly involved in production. I enjoy being involved throughout the engineering process, asking questions, understanding challenges firsthand, and finding opportunities to improve both products and processes.

As I continue developing as an engineer, I'm excited to pursue opportunities that allow me to combine creative design, practical problem-solving, and hands-on manufacturing experience.
